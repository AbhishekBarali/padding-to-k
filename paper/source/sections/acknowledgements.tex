\section*{AI-use statement}

We used an AI assistant (Kiro, running Claude Opus 5.5) under our direction throughout this project.
Author A conceived the study, chose the research question, set or approved every design decision, reviewed the assistant's work daily, and gave the feedback that shaped the plan, the pilot and this paper.
Every human judgement in the study is ours: we set the counting rules and settled the cases they left open, author B checked every cleanup decision by hand, we did the blind checks, author A checked 65 scored outputs and author B recounted 70 by hand, and we each labelled 400 entries independently and settled our differences together.
Under our direction, the assistant did the routine work: it wrote and tested the code, drafted rules and the matcher's development labels under our guidance, applied our counting rules to the candidate lists for our check, ran and logged the experiments, checked each citation against the cited paper, and drafted text, which we read and revised through our feedback.
No AI output was used as a human label, and the matcher is judged only against our labels.
No Claude model is among the models tested.
We take full responsibility for the content; \texttt{AI-USE.md} in the repository records each use of the assistant and how its results were checked.
